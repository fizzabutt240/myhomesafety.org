Questions, answered

Before you book.

Can you help with more than one certificate?

Yes. Landlord and property compliance requirements can be grouped into a coordinated package, depending on the property and services required.

What happens if an inspection identifies a problem?

Where appropriate, follow-on repairs, remedial works, upgrades or installations can be discussed so you have a practical route forward.

Do you work with domestic and commercial properties?

Yes. The service scope includes domestic and commercial properties, as well as HMOs, new builds and older properties.

Are urgent appointments available?

Fast appointments, priority bookings and selected emergency callouts may be available, subject to the service requirements and engineer availability.

Which areas do you cover?

Our primary coverage area is London within the M25. We can also consider work in other UK cities depending on the volume of work required.

Which details help you review an enquiry?

Include the property postcode, property type, service needed, access arrangements and preferred timing. For portfolios, include the number of properties and their locations.
